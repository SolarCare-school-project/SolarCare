## Expansion Path & Scalability

We start with private customers. Once the product works reliably, it expands to larger customers with higher system value and higher willingness to pay.

| Phase | Segment | Why this segment | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 – Launch** | Private homeowners (5–15 kWp) | Large market, simple systems | Year 1 |
| **2 – Extension** | Multi-family buildings, owner associations, farmers (30–100 kWp) | Bigger systems, higher loss per outage | Year 2–3 |
| **3 – Expansion** | Businesses, schools, municipalities, larger institutions (100 kWp+) | Professional operators, higher budgets, B2B contracts. Safer network. | Year 3+ |

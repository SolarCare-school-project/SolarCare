## Target Group

**Primary target group:** Private homeowners with a rooftop PV system.

| Criterion | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Customer type** | Private households (B2C) |
| **Property** | Single- or two-family home, owner-occupied |
| **PV system** | Rooftop system, 5–15 kWp, often with battery storage |
| **System age** | 3–12 years (warranties expiring, inverter replacement approaching) |
| **Connectivity** | Inverter connected to home Wi-Fi |
| **Region** | 1. DACH region (first approach)<br>2. Europe (second approach)<br>3. Global (third approach) |
